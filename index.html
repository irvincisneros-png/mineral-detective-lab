About this laboratory

Specimen images are AI-generated educational illustrations with photographic detail, not photographs of measured physical samples. Turning an image changes its viewing angle; it is not a full 3D scan. Identify specimens using multiple properties.

Mass and displacement are linked by density. Non-aqueous displacement liquid is used for water-soluble specimens. Scratch tools have stated model hardness values; real materials vary. Quartz can scratch porcelain instead of leaving a useful mineral streak.

The dilute hydrochloric acid test is simulated. Real practical work requires teacher supervision and appropriate protection.

References: National Park Service: Mohs scale; University of Minnesota: Common Minerals; Halite identification.
